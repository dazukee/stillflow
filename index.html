Stillflow

Make a photo move.

Draw where things should flow, freeze what should stay still, and get a seamless loop. Your photo never leaves this browser.

or drop an image anywhere, or paste one with Ctrl+V

  1. Draw paths along water, clouds, smoke
  2. Freeze anything that should hold still
  3. Export an MP4 or GIF that loops forever

Stillflow needs WebGL 2, and this browser doesn't seem to have it. A recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari will work.
